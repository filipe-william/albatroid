2025/2026 Unearthed

In the FIRST® LEGO® League UNEARTHED℠ season, we were invited to explore a fascinating universe full of mysteries: archaeology. This enchanting theme takes us on a time-travel journey to dive into human history through the discovery, excavation, and study of ancient artifacts. Within the Innovation Project area, the challenge is to contribute creatively and effectively to the work of archaeologists. This can be done by improving an existing technology or creating a completely new solution that facilitates the various stages of the archaeological process. The possibilities are vast: from developing more precise tools for the excavation of archaeological sites, creating innovative methods for the conservation of historical pieces, to finding more accessible and interactive ways to study and share knowledge about ancient civilizations with the world. Above all, the season invites us to unearth the past to build the future, combining science, technology, and imagination with a commitment to preserving human history and culture.

Our Journey

Regional

Today marks more than a week since our tournament, an important regional tournament full of incredible memories. Congratulations to all the teams that participated in the Sesi DF Regional Tournament, you are all amazing! And a great achievement happened: seeing our three teams - Albatroid, Atomics, and Aion-X - together at a national event, all of this being the fruit of our effort and hard work. We would like to honor and thank our coaches, André Mota and João Matheus, as well as our mentors Filipe William and Gabriel Antunes, for supporting us throughout our journey. After six years, we managed to bring the First Regional Champions award back to Albatroid, and it was a very beautiful process, full of ups and downs. But, with dedication, we managed to overcome these obstacles, always remembering that when we work together, we are stronger. After all, we are a family, and our friendship is worth more than anything! We thank Júlia Maria for joining at a time when we really needed it, for supporting us and adapting so well. To Julia Cristina, for being an incredible captain who understands us and is always very helpful. To Isabela Brito, our youngest, who brings joy and energy to the team every day! To Matheus, our engineer, a person with a huge heart and great charisma. To João Vitor, who always makes us laugh and brings confidence to the team. And to Samuel, who unfortunately will not be with us at the national event for personal reasons — just like Júlia Maria —, but who helped us a lot in the process and were extremely important. Thank you so much to everyone who is part of this very important stage for Albatroid, highlighting our sister teams: Atomics and Aion-X. This regional was just the beginning of an incredible season.

National

This FLL season has been one of the most challenging we could have ever imagined. We are all new, starting from zero, learning everything in practice. Falling down, but getting back up and trying again. And yet, with all the difficulties, with every meeting, every laugh, every crazy idea that turned into a solution, we felt we were on the right track.

We are not just a team. We are a family. Each with our own way, our skills, our fears... and our courage. Together, we understand that making mistakes is part of it. That asking for help is not a weakness. And that when we think about each other, we go much further.

This season is not about winning or losing. It's about growing. About looking back and seeing how much we've evolved, even in such a short time. About being proud of what we built with our own hands and hearts. Once an Albatroid, always an Albatroid!
